Data-to-Dirt: Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 2026

By using Data-to-Dirt, students and educational institutions agree to participate in verified sustainability challenges. File cleanup requests are subject to pedagogical oversight by assigned classroom educators.

1. Acceptable Use

Users must submit only genuine, unneeded digital assets for cleanup. Deliberate file spamming or artificial bloating to manipulate leaderboard standings violates community guidelines.

2. Educator Oversight

All permanent deletion operations require teacher verification to prevent unintended data loss.
